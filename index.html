Discover the wonders of astronomy

Astronomy is the study of everything beyond our planet —
from the planets and moons of our solar system to distant stars, galaxies,
and the vastness of the universe.
Through observation, research, and space exploration,
scientists continue to uncover the secrets of the cosmos and
deepen our understanding of where we come from and what lies beyond.
