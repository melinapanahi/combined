About The Cosmos Museum

Where curiosity meets the stars.

Our Story

The Cosmos Museum was founded with a single mission: to bring the wonders of the universe closer to everyone. What started as a small planetarium exhibit has grown into a full-scale museum dedicated to space exploration, astronomy, and the mysteries that lie beyond our planet.

Our Mission

We believe that understanding the universe is understanding ourselves. Our goal is to inspire visitors of all ages to look up, ask questions, and explore the science behind the stars, planets, and galaxies that surround us.

What You'll Find Here

From interactive exhibits on our solar system to detailed models of distant planets, The Cosmos Museum offers an immersive journey through space. Visitors can explore each planet in detail, learn about their unique characteristics, and discover how they change throughout the year.

Our Values

Curiosity

We nurture questions before answers, and wonder before facts.

Education

Science should be accessible, engaging, and open to everyone.

Discovery

Every exhibit is designed to spark a new interest in the unknown.
